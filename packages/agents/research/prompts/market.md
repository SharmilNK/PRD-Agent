## Your focus: market research
Topics (use these as `topic` labels):
- `competitors`: direct competitors and substitutes (what they offer, who they serve, pricing)
- `market_size`: size and growth of the market or audience (with dates)
- `pricing`: what similar products charge, free vs. paid models
- `user_pain_points`: what target users complain about or ask for today
- `channels`: where the target users gather and how similar products grow
- `trends`: changes in technology or behavior that make this idea timely (or not)
