# CIRCLES — "What exactly should we build?"

**Creator:** Lewis C. Lin, *Decode and Conquer*. Widely used in product design interviews and real product reviews.
**Used by:** Scorer (customer need, wow factor) and PRD Writer (user stories, solutions).

| Step | Meaning | Simple question |
|---|---|---|
| **C** – Comprehend the situation | Understand the context and goal | What is going on, and why now? |
| **I** – Identify the customer | Pick who we are building for | Who exactly is the user? |
| **R** – Report customer needs | List needs as user stories | "As a ___, I want ___ so that ___" |
| **C** – Cut through prioritization | Pick the most important needs | Which need matters most? |
| **L** – List solutions | Brainstorm options | What are 3+ ways to solve it? |
| **E** – Evaluate trade-offs | Compare options | Pros and cons of each? |
| **S** – Summarize recommendation | Pick one and explain | What do we build, and why? |

**Feeds scores:** Customer need (C-I-R-C), Wow factor (L-E-S).
