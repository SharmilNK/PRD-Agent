# Value Proposition Canvas — "What value do we give?"

**Creator:** Alexander Osterwalder, Strategyzer.
**Used by:** Scorer (value).

| Customer side | Product side |
|---|---|
| **Customer jobs** — what they are trying to do | **Products & services** — what we offer |
| **Pains** — what annoys or blocks them | **Pain relievers** — how we remove pains |
| **Gains** — what they hope to get | **Gain creators** — how we create gains |

**Fit** = our pain relievers and gain creators match their top pains and gains.
