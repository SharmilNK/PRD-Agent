# 7 Powers — "What stops others from copying us?"

**Creator:** Hamilton Helmer, *7 Powers: The Foundations of Business Strategy* (2016).
**Used by:** Scorer (defensibility). Especially important for AI products, because big AI labs can ship similar features quickly.

| Power | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Scale economies** | Cost per unit drops as we grow |
| **Network economies** | Product gets better as more people use it |
| **Counter-positioning** | A new model incumbents can't copy without hurting themselves |
| **Switching costs** | Hard for users to leave |
| **Branding** | People pay more because they trust the name |
| **Cornered resource** | Exclusive access to something valuable (data, talent, IP) |
| **Process power** | A way of working that is hard to copy |
