# Google HEART — "How do we measure user experience?"

**Creator:** Kerry Rodden, Hilary Hutchinson, Xin Fu — Google (2010).
**Used by:** Scorer (usability) and PRD Writer (success metrics).

| Letter | Meaning | Example metric (newsletter) |
|---|---|---|
| **H**appiness | Satisfaction | Reader rating per issue, NPS |
| **E**ngagement | Depth of use | Open rate, scroll depth, quiz completion |
| **A**doption | New users | New subscribers per week |
| **R**etention | Users who come back | 4-week retention of subscribers |
| **T**ask success | Can they do the main task? | % who pass the Basic quiz |

Use with **Goals → Signals → Metrics**: pick a goal, find a signal of it, turn it into a number.
