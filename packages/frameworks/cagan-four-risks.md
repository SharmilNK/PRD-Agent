# Marty Cagan's Four Big Risks — "Can this succeed?"

**Creator:** Marty Cagan, Silicon Valley Product Group (SVPG), *Inspired* (2nd ed.). Standard at many tech companies.
**Used by:** Scorer (usability, feasibility, viability, value).

| Risk | Question |
|---|---|
| **Value risk** | Will customers buy it or choose to use it? |
| **Usability risk** | Can users figure out how to use it? |
| **Feasibility risk** | Can our engineers build it with the time, skills and technology we have? |
| **Business viability risk** | Does it work for our business — cost, sales, legal, brand? |

Note: the founder's original scoring list (need, value, usability, feasibility, viability) maps almost one-to-one onto these four risks.
