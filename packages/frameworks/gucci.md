# GUCCI — "Should we build this?"

**Creator:** Dr. Nancy Li (product strategy and PM case interviews).
**Used by:** the Scorer agent, *first*, as the strategy lens.

| Letter | Question to answer | Evidence we expect |
|---|---|---|
| **G – Goals & Mission** | Does the idea fit the company's (or creator's) mission, vision and goals (growth, market share, engagement)? | A stated mission or goal, and how this idea moves it |
| **U – Unmet Needs** | What real, specific customer problem or market gap exists? | Quotes, data, search trends, forum posts — not guesses |
| **C – Competition** | Red ocean (crowded) or blue ocean (open)? What is our unique advantage? | Named competitors and how we differ |
| **C – Customer Segmentation** | Which specific, underserved or niche group will adopt first? | A named early-adopter segment and why they care most |
| **I – Integrated Ecosystem** | Does it work with existing products, platforms, partners, tech stack? | The channels, tools or products it plugs into |

**For a solo creator:** "company mission" = your personal goals; "ecosystem" = your existing channels (LinkedIn, GitHub, blog, Substack, audience).

**Feeds scores:** Viability (G), Customer need (U, C-seg), Competitors (C), Feasibility + Defensibility (I).
