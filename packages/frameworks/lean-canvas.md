# Lean Canvas + TAM/SAM/SOM + AARRR — "Can it make money and grow?"

**Lean Canvas creator:** Ash Maurya (*Running Lean*), adapted from Alexander Osterwalder's Business Model Canvas.
**Used by:** Scorer (viability) and Market agent.

**Lean Canvas boxes:** Problem · Customer segments · Unique value proposition · Solution · Channels · Revenue streams · Cost structure · Key metrics · Unfair advantage.

**Market size:**
- **TAM** (Total Addressable Market) — everyone who could ever use it
- **SAM** (Serviceable Available Market) — the part we can reach
- **SOM** (Serviceable Obtainable Market) — the part we can realistically win soon

**AARRR "pirate metrics"** (Dave McClure): Acquisition → Activation → Retention → Referral → Revenue.
