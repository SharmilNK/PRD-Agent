# Porter's Five Forces — "How tough is this market?"

**Creator:** Michael E. Porter, Harvard Business School (1979).
**Used by:** Scorer (competitors) and Market agent.

| Force | Question |
|---|---|
| **Rivalry among competitors** | How many players, and how hard do they fight? |
| **Threat of new entrants** | How easy is it for someone new to start? |
| **Threat of substitutes** | What else solves the same job (books, YouTube, courses)? |
| **Buyer power** | Can customers easily switch or demand lower prices? |
| **Supplier power** | Do we depend on a few suppliers (e.g., one AI model provider, one platform)? |

Combine with a simple **SWOT** (Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, Threats) and a competitor matrix.
