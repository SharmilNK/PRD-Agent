# Where the PRD template comes from

**Honest note:** the exact internal PRD templates of Google, Meta, Microsoft and Adobe are mostly **not public**. This template is built from **public** sources that those companies (or well-known product leaders) have published or described. Each PRD section names its source.

| Source | Who | What we borrow | Sections |
|---|---|---|---|
| Working Backwards PR/FAQ | Amazon (described by Colin Bryar & Bill Carr, *Working Backwards*, 2021) | Start with a press release + FAQ | 1 |
| "How to write a good PRD" / *Inspired* | Marty Cagan, SVPG | Problem before solution; Four Big Risks | 2, 16, 17 |
| GUCCI framework | Dr. Nancy Li | Strategic fit | 3 |
| Design docs | Google (publicly described by Google engineers) | Goals / non-goals, alternatives considered | 6, 14 |
| HEART framework | Google (Rodden, Hutchinson, Fu, 2010) | UX metrics | 6 |
| PRD template collection | Lenny Rachitsky (templates shared by Figma, Asana, Intercom and others) | Section order, user stories | 7 |
| RICE | Intercom | Prioritization | 8 |
| People + AI Guidebook | Google PAIR | AI-fit, explainability, trust | 9, 12 |
| Model Cards | Google (Mitchell et al., 2019) | Documenting model use and limits | 9, 12 |
| OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications | OWASP | AI security risks | 10 |
| Responsible AI Standard v2 + Impact Assessment | Microsoft | Responsible AI section | 11 |
| AI Risk Management Framework | NIST | Risk section | 11, 17 |
| EU AI Act | European Union | Risk level, transparency duties | 11 |
| Architecture Decision Records | Michael Nygard | Decision log | 19 |

In Phase 3 the research agents will fetch these sources live and add links into each generated PRD.
